Subject: Question on split supercuspidal places in Castella–Liu–Wan and Fouquet–Wan, Cor. 7.28

Dear Professor Wan,

I am an amateur working through the supersingular p-adic converse theorems in Burungale–Skinner–Tian–Wan (arXiv 2409.01350, Thms. 9.24 and 10.5). These go through your work with Castella and Liu (arXiv 2109.08375). The aim is to see whether the square-free hypothesis on N can be relaxed at primes of additive reduction that are split in the auxiliary field K, typically 2 and 3, where π_v is supercuspidal. I have a short question about the local triple product integrals at such places.

In Fouquet–Wan (arXiv 2107.13726, Prop. 7.27 and Cor. 7.28), you compute I_ℓ(φ_ℓ ⊗ f_{χθ} ⊗ f_{χh}) = Vol(K_{t1}) for π_ℓ supercuspidal of conductor ℓ^t, φ_ℓ the new vector, and auxiliary characters with t1 > t such that χ_{h,1}χ_{θ,1} and χ_{h,2}χ_{θ,2} are unramified.

1. In Castella–Liu–Wan, the auxiliary characters at v ∈ Σ_s are fixed by condition (iv) of §5.6, and the form is twisted by (5.6.10). Would it be enough, at a split place where π_v is supercuspidal, to replace (iv) by the condition of your Cor. 7.28 and to use the new vector instead of the twist (5.6.10)? In other words, is condition (iv) used anywhere else in the paper (for instance in Lemma 6.24 of your ANT paper, or in the choice of the p-adic L-functions in condition (vi)) in a way that would rule this out?

2. In Cor. 7.28, should "conductors ℓ^{t1}" be read as cond(χ_{θ,1}χ_{θ,2}^{-1}) = cond(χ_{h,1}χ_{h,2}^{-1}) = ℓ^{t1}?

3. A small consistency remark, which I suspect comes from my misreading of the conventions. Suppose f_{χθ} and f_{χh} are both K_0(ℓ^a)-eigenvectors with the characters of §8D. Then the functional φ ↦ ℓ(φ ⊗ f_{χθ} ⊗ f_{χh}) vanishes on all of π unless c(π ⊗ χ_{θ,1}χ_{h,1}) ≤ a. This is a Casselman-type argument, and I checked it numerically for GL2(Z/3^n). Your choice in Cor. 7.28 satisfies this criterion. However, I could not reconcile it with the parameters of Lemma 8.17 of arXiv 1408.4044 (t1 + s = t2 with t1 < s). Are the θ and h vectors at split places perhaps of opposite orientation (K_0 versus its transpose) there? A small numerical check (p = 3, t1 = 1, s = 2, t2 = 3), with f_{χ,ϑ}(b·n⁻(y)) = χ(b)δ^{1/2}(b)ϑ(y/p^{t1})·1[v(y) = t1], gives ⟨π(n(b))f_{χ,ϑ}, f̃⟩ = −1/9 ≠ 0 for b a unit. This seems to contradict the support statement of Lemma 8.14, and the projection of f_{χ,ϑ} to the (K_0(p^{t2}), Ξ^{-1})-type vanishes. I am very likely misreading your definition of f_{χ,ϑ}, and would be grateful for a correction.

Thank you very much for your time. Any short pointer would be greatly appreciated.
